\## BARISTA AND CUSTOMER ORDERING SYSTEM

\## Overview

Third Culture Coffee is a coffee shop where customers order

food and beverages through staff/baristas. During our analysis,

we studied the existing ordering process by talking to

customers and staff and observing how orders are taken,

punched into the system, paid for, and sent to the relevant

staff for preparation.

The current system allows staff to select items from predefined

item tags and generate two order slips, one for customer and

one for staff. However, our analysis identified some difficulties

related to seasonal menu items, item availability, discounts,

and order punching.

Customers mainly discover information about Third Culture

through Instagram, Google Maps, peers, and personal visits.

Regular customers are familiar with the menu, while new

customers may need more guidance about available items and

different flavors.

Therefore, our proposed system aims to make the ordering

process faster, easier and more convenient for both

customers and staff.

\##Problem Definition

PROBLEM OF: The current system does not provide

a sufficiently organized and updated way to manage

seasonal items, item availability, and customer

orders.

EFFECTS: This can cause delays, confusion, and

difficulty

IMPACT OF WHICH TO: Both staff and customer.

SOLUTION: Distinct menus according to seasons in

punching system.



\## Scope

\- In scope: Seasonal Menu Management

\- In scope: Item Availability Management

\- In scope: Easy Order Punching

\- In scope: Customer Recommendations

\- Out of scope: Full inventory management.

\- Out of scope: Managing the entire financial system

\## Nonfunctional requirements

\- NFR-01: For 100 stored tasks, the task list shall appear within

2 seconds in at least 95 of 100 trials on the agreed test device.

\- NFR-02: All task actions shall be operable using a keyboard

alone, verified against each documented task workflow.

\## Acceptance criteria

\- FR-01: Given the title \&quot;Read Chapter 1\&quot;, saving a new task

causes that title to appear in the task list.

\- FR-02: With two saved tasks, opening the list shows both titles.

\- FR-03: Marking an incomplete task complete changes its displayed

status to Complete.
